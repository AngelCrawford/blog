# Engineering Backlog

This backlog collects cross-cutting or future action items that emerge from reviews and planning.

Routing guidance:

- Use this file for non-urgent optimizations, refactors, or follow-ups that span multiple stories/epics.
- Must-fix items to ship a story belong in that story's `Tasks / Subtasks`.
- Same-epic improvements may also be captured under the epic Tech Spec `Post-Review Follow-ups` section.
- Items routed to a not-yet-drafted future story belong in `docs/1-planning/epics.md` under that story as a **Pre-Spec Notes** entry — not here.
- **Done items are pruned, not preserved** — git log and GitHub PRs/issues are the historical record.
- **When pruning a row that originated from a story review, also update the originating entry in `docs/sprint-artifacts/epic-*/X-Y-*.md`**: change the `[Review][Defer]` tag to `[Review][Defer→Fixed YYYY-MM-DD]` and append a one-line resolution summary. The Story field in the row tells you which file to grep. Skip if no `[Review][Defer]` entry exists (e.g. cross-cutting items with `—` in the Story column).
