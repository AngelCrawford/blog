## Vision Statement

> Create a digital space where knowledge grows organically, quality content naturally rises to the surface, and continuous refinement is rewarded over constant publishing.

## Core Philosophy

### Digital Garden Principles

1. **Growth Over Publishing** - Tending existing content is as valuable as creating new
2. **Quality Over Recency** - Old but evergreen beats new but shallow
3. **Transparency Over Polish** - Show the messy middle, learning in public
4. **Community Over Metrics** - Federated engagement, not vanity stats
5. **Intentionality Over Scrolling** - Three-tier mental model, not infinite feeds

### Anti-Patterns We're Rejecting

- ❌ Infinite scroll (doom-scrolling)
- ❌ Publish-and-forget mentality
- ❌ Newest = most visible
- ❌ Tracking cookies and surveillance
- ❌ Vanity metrics (views, likes without context)

## Target Outcomes

### For Angel (Content Creator)

- Motivated to update old articles (grace period visibility)
- Can mark content maturity (seedling → evergreen)
- Transparent about content state (draft → polished)
- Track evolution over time (history field)
- Curate quality via manual weight (0-10)
- **Diverse content types** (articles, videos, galleries, projects)

### For Readers

- Discover quality content by popularity, not date
- See content maturity at a glance (growth badges)
- Trust evergreen content is maintained
- Know when content is withered/deprecated
- Engage without cookies (anonymous hearts)
- **Explore different formats** (text, video, images, projects)

### For the Web

- IndieWeb-compliant (own your data)
- Federated (webmentions, POSSE to Mastodon/Threads)
- Privacy-respecting (no tracking)
- Accessible (progressive enhancement)
- Sustainable (static generation)

## User Personas

### Primary: Angel (Content Creator)

**Who:** Full-stack web developer, blogger, digital garden enthusiast
**Goals:**
- Maintain valuable content over time
- Build a sustainable writing practice
- Learn in public with transparency
- Experiment with different content formats

**Pain Points:**
- Old articles become invisible
- No motivation to update existing content
- Lack of engagement signals (no invasive analytics)
- Limited to text-only format

**How Digital Garden Helps:**
- Updates trigger visibility boost (grace period)
- Growth stages make maturity explicit
- Anonymous hearts provide engagement signals
- 6 formats support diverse creativity

### Secondary: Anna (Engaged Reader)

**Who:** Tech-savvy professional, values quality over quantity
**Goals:**
- Find in-depth, well-maintained resources
- Discover hidden gems (not just latest posts)
- Understand content quality/maturity
- Engage without privacy concerns

**Pain Points:**
- Valuable old content buried in archives
- No indication if content is current/deprecated
- Tracking cookies violate privacy
- Text-heavy blogs feel monotonous

**How Digital Garden Helps:**
- Quality sorting surfaces best content
- Growth stage badges signal maturity
- Zero tracking, anonymous hearts
- Visual variety (galleries, videos)

### Tertiary: Markus (Casual Visitor)

**Who:** Arrives via Google search, quick information seeker
**Goals:**
- Find specific answer fast
- Assess content trustworthiness
- Navigate without JavaScript if needed

**Pain Points:**
- Outdated tutorials waste time
- No indication of content freshness
- Heavy JavaScript breaks experience

**How Digital Garden Helps:**
- Withered stage warns of deprecation
- Last update date visible
- Progressive enhancement (works without JS)
- Clear growth stage signals quality
