Marks how ripe an entry is — the one piece of digital-garden vocabulary on the site.

```jsx
<GrowthBadge stage="evergreen" />
<GrowthBadge stage="withered" showLabel />
```

Four stages: `seedling` (lime, früher Entwurf), `budding` (magenta, in Entwicklung), `evergreen` (green, gepflegt & aktuell), `withered` (grey, veraltet). Icon-only in card footers, with the English label visible in the article sidebar. The German tooltip is always attached. Never tint a surface or a run of text with a growth colour — they belong to icons alone.
