Sits at the top of an article whose growth stage is `withered` — the garden's honesty mechanism.

```jsx
<WitheredBanner
  date="12.03.24"
  reason="Bulma ist hier inzwischen durch Tailwind ersetzt."
  replacement="Die Migration nach Tailwind" replacementHref="/articles/tailwind/"
  onDismiss={fn}
/>
```

Warning-yellow left edge, grey skull glyph (the same one the `withered` growth badge uses, so the two read as one family). Dismiss target is 44px so it is reachable on a phone.
